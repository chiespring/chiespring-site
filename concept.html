CONCEPT

We feel that information sharing is not progressing in business and society in general because of the following obstacles:

  • Work that machines and AI should be doing, work with little meaning in itself, along with its methods and results, is treated as if it were confidential.
  • While defining life and career purpose is the essential role of human beings, society lacks the time, cultural space, and foundational ground to engage in open dialogue and vibrant debate on such matters.

Trapped by these constraints, individuals become physically and mentally depleted by tasks unrelated to the skills they genuinely ought to cultivate. Consequently, they lose the bandwidth to develop core capabilities over time, learn from others, resolve personal struggles, or re-evaluate their approaches.

In response, Chiespring shares practical wisdom grounded in the following foundational principles:

  • Sharing tools and datasets that promote and streamline entrusting administrative work to machines and AI, freeing people from that work.
  • Sharing knowledge and wisdom that help build the mindset needed to pursue the purposes people are meant to take on.
  • Sharing stories of life experience told by people, to help build the mindset of trying to understand others.
